Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Brydan Carey (creator and owner of "Truckin All Day") respects your privacy. We collect minimal information to ensure the functionality of our mobile application and web platform. The data we collect includes:

  • Local Game Data: High scores and game progress are stored locally on your device using localStorage or UserDefaults. We do not transmit this data to our servers.
  • Crash Reports: We may optionally collect anonymous crash diagnostics to improve stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is solely used to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • Save your arcade progression.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app contains embedded players and links to third-party streaming services (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Amazon, Deezer) and a merch store (Fourthwall). These services have their own privacy policies, and we do not share your data with them directly.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@truckinallday.com.
